# Product Guardrails

- Hobby agnostic
- Secure by default
- Build in milestones
- The collection is the primary product unit
- Keep collection identity visible in every collection-owned workflow
- Optimize for organizing and enjoying collections, not administrative completeness
- Preserve a relational catalog model with controlled customization
- Treat presentation and sharing as product capabilities
- Keep additional client apps out of scope until the web experience proves the need
