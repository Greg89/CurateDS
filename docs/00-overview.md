# Collection Curator Overview

API-backed web catalog platform for flexible personal collections.

## V2 Direction

CurateDS V2 is a collection-first experience for hobbyists who want to organize, enjoy, and show what they collect. The product is intentionally single-user and multi-collection: each collection is a complete personal workspace with its own identity, catalog, insights, and future showcase.
